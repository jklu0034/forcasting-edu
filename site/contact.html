Common Questions

Quick Answers
How do we link our two accounts? expand_more

After enrolling, the first partner receives an invite link to share. Once the second partner signs up with it, your progress and evening dialogues sync automatically.

Can we switch pathways later? expand_more

Yes. If a track feels too fast or too gentle within the first two weeks, write to us and we’ll move you both at no cost.

Is the licence really one price for two? expand_more

Always. Every pathway is a single one-time payment per couple, with two synced logins and two workbooks included.
